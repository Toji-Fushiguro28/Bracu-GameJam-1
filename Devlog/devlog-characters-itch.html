A new face at the desk, and something in the walls

This update lets you choose who runs the Whitmore. It also hides something new in the library. We'll tell you all about the first, and nothing about the second.

Choose your librarian

Lots of you asked for a librarian who looks a bit more like you. When you start a new game, the page where you write your name now has a second page beside it, with your librarian standing on it.

  • Use the ◀ ▶ arrows to switch between the two librarians. They give a little wave each time.
  • Your name appears under them as you type it.
  • Press That's me and that's who sits in the chair at the interview and runs the library from then on.

Both librarians share the same skeleton, so every animation still works: carrying stacks, stamping cards, typing at the computer and sitting down to read. Only the body changes. Nobody in the game calls you "he" or "she", so every line of dialogue fits whoever you pick.

Saves from before this update keep the librarian you already had. Nothing is lost.

Something has changed at the Whitmore

We won't say what. We won't even say where.

Look closely at the old things in the library. Read everything you find. And maybe don't rush out the door the moment the bell rings.

If you find it, please keep it to yourself. Let the next librarian find it too.

Also in this update

  • No surprise first look: the name and character page is already up before the intro fades in, so you never see the room before you've chosen.
  • Even lighting on the preview: your librarian's face is lit cleanly on the picker, with no strange dark shadows across the chin.
  • The ropes and the night: a few small fixes to what happens after closing time. That's all we'll say.

Thank you for playing. The Whitmore keeps a few secrets. Now it keeps one more.
